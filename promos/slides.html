Caught before it is filed

A rate the rule cannot carry.

sevDesk accepts only certain rates under each rule. An export that charged 19% is a Commerce tax problem, and Sevvies says so.

  • Rule 1 · 0, 7 or 19%
  • Ausfuhren, §19, reverse charge · 0% only
  • One Stop Shop · the destination decides
Needs attention

This order charged 19% VAT, but sevDesk only accepts 0% under “Ausfuhren”. Check the Commerce tax rules for this destination.

Nothing was sent. The order stays in Commerce, untouched, until someone decides which of the two is wrong.
